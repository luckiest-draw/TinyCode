# Returns and refunds policy

This document is a fictional store policy written for demo and testing purposes.

## Return window

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The item must be in
its original condition and include the original packaging and accessories.

## How to start a return

To start a return, the customer opens a return request for the delivered
order and selects the items to return. A return label is provided once the
request is approved.

## Refund timing

Once the returned item is received and inspected, the refund is issued to the
original payment method. Refunds generally appear within 3 to 7 business
days after the return is accepted.

## Non-returnable items

The following items cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or
defective: personalized or custom-made items, opened digital content, and
items marked as final sale.

## Damaged or incorrect items

If an item arrives damaged, defective, or different from what was ordered,
the customer should contact support within 48 hours of delivery with the
order id and photos. The item may be replaced or refunded, including the
shipping cost.
