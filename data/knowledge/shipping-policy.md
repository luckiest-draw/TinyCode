# Shipping and delivery policy

This document is a fictional store policy written for demo and testing purposes.

## Processing time

Orders are prepared and handed to the carrier within 1 to 2 business days
after payment is approved. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are
processed on the next business day.

## Delivery estimates

Estimated delivery is shown at checkout and is based on the shipping zone:

- Same-region shipments: 3 to 5 business days.
- Cross-region shipments: 5 to 9 business days.

Delivery estimates are not guaranteed delivery dates.

## Tracking

Once an order ships, the customer receives a shipment notification. Delivery
updates reflect the current status of the shipment. If a shipment is marked
as delayed, the customer can request an updated estimate from support.

## Late and missing deliveries

A shipment is considered late when it has not arrived by the end of the
estimated delivery window. If an order is not delivered within 5 business
days after the latest estimated date, the customer may contact support for a
refund of the shipping cost or a replacement shipment.

## Failed delivery

If a delivery attempt fails because the address is unreachable, the carrier
will retry. If delivery continues to fail, the order may be returned to the
seller and the customer will be contacted to arrange redelivery or a refund.
