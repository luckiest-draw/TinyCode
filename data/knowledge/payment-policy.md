# Payment policy

This document is a fictional store policy written for demo and testing purposes.

## Accepted payment methods

The store accepts credit cards, debit cards, digital wallets, bank transfers,
and store gift cards. Prices are listed and charged in USD.

## Authorization

At checkout the payment method is authorized for the order total. The charge
is captured when the order is approved. If an order is cancelled before
shipment, the authorization or charge is released and refunded.

## Installments

Credit and debit card payments may be split into installments where the
issuing bank supports it. Installment plans do not change the order total.

## Failed payments

If a payment is declined, the order stays in processing and the customer is
asked to retry with another payment method. An order is not prepared for
shipment until payment is approved.

## Gift cards

Gift card balances are applied before other payment methods. Any refund for
a gift-card purchase is returned to the gift card balance.
